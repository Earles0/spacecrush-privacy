SPACECRUSH

Privacy & Support

Effective November 1, 2026 · Android app: com.earlesgames.spacecrush

Your game progress stays with your device.

Spacecrush is an offline, single-player matching game. The current release has no account registration, ads, in-app purchases, online leaderboards, or developer-operated cloud saves.

Who we are

This policy covers the Spacecrush Android game published by Spacecrush Studio. For privacy questions or player support, contact earlessilver@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

The current release does not send personal information, gameplay events, identifiers, or game saves to us or to an analytics or advertising service. Unity Analytics, Unity cloud diagnostics, and online leaderboard services are not active in this release.

The game stores information on your device so it can work and remember your choices, including:

  • Saved runs, scores, achievements, earned coins, and unlocked items.
  • Audio, vibration, graphics, language, and tutorial preferences.
  • A locally generated installation identifier used with your local leaderboard records. It is not an online account and is not sent to a leaderboard server.

Spacecrush does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. Vibration is used only for optional gameplay feedback.

Sharing and platform services

We do not sell personal information or share game data with advertising networks. Google Play and Android may separately process installation, store, security, backup, or diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings. Platform-provided crash and performance reports may be available to us through Google Play Console. See Google’s privacy policy.

Android may include app data in device backups or device-to-device transfers, depending on your settings and platform behavior. We do not operate that backup service or have access to your private backups.

Keeping and deleting your data

Local game data remains until you clear the app’s storage, uninstall it, or remove it using your device’s controls. Clearing storage removes game progress and settings. Android may restore backed-up app data after reinstallation; manage those backups through your Google account and device settings if you want to remove them too.

There is no Spacecrush account to delete and no developer-operated copy of your game save. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to answer your request, keep it only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations, and can review a deletion request sent to the same contact address. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Age audience

Spacecrush is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address it.

About this webpage

This page has no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or embedded third-party fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening an email link uses your chosen email application.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Features such as online services, advertising, purchases, or analytics would require a fresh privacy review and updated disclosures before release.
